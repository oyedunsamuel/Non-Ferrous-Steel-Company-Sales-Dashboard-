An interactive, end-to-end Power BI analytics solution designed to monitor global sales performance, product profitability, and freight efficiency for a commercial steel manufacturing enterprise. 

This project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence, giving executive leadership and logistics managers dynamic visibility into product margins, shipping overhead, and account retention.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Business Problem

Commercial steel manufacturing relies on high sales volumes, but operates on tight margins, volatile material pricing, and heavy transportation overhead. 

Key operational challenges addressed by this project include:
* **Margin Blind Spots:** High sales volume obscured which specific steel grades (e.g., hot-rolled coil vs. structural beams) were driving net profitability versus low-margin volume.
* **Freight Cost Leakage:** Unmonitored shipping corridors were eroding net margins on long-distance shipments.
* **Reporting Bottlenecks:** Monthly reviews relied on static spreadsheets, delaying tactical sales and supply chain pivots.

---

## 🏗️ Data Architecture & Star Schema

The project utilizes a **Star Schema** to optimize query performance, maintain clean filter propagation, and support seamless DAX calculations.
